Mapping Seasons Across Climates
Published 31 December 2025

Precipitation and temperature data shown comes from CHELSA Climate Datasets.
The green line on the plot shows average Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) from MODIS/Terra, with values that may be cloud cover removed. The data is scaled logarithmically, with a baseline of 1000. I did not include a scale on the y axis for this because it is rather fuzzy, and the quantities don't directly translate to a measurable value on the ground.

Press number keys to change between overlaid maps:
1: Mean Precipitation
2: Biennial Wet Seasons
3: Wet to Dry Ratio
4: Wettest Month
5: Summer-Winter Temperature Inversion
6: Wet Season Duration
