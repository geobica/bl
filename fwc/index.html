Flags with Constellations
Published 7 July 2026

Above is the cool new flag constellation editor I've been working on, in a similar way to [the US flag star modifying tool][/bl/usf]. This one lets you drag around a celestial globe and modify cutoffs for the correspondance between the sizes of stars in constellations on flags and the magnitudes of those stars in real life.
As an example, the flag of Australia has the Southern Cross on it, with Alpha through Delta Crucis[properly Acrux, Mimosa, Gacrux, and Imai] depicted as larger seven pointed stars, while Epsilon Crucis is depicted with a smaller five pointed star. On the left though is another even larger seven pointed star located relative to Crux in a direction where Beta Centauri would be found. However, the official Australian Flag booklet never mentions Beta Centauri, merely calling it the "Commonwealth Star".
